The Founder

Stefano
Osuji

Founder & CEO · Fano AI

Stefano Osuji spent years competing as a professional basketball player — a world where split-second decisions, team coordination under pressure, and peak cognitive performance are the difference between winning and losing. That environment gave him something most technologists never have: a lived understanding of how humans perform when the stakes are real.

After his playing career, Stefano pursued and earned a Doctorate in Industrial-Organisational Psychology — the scientific discipline that studies how people think, decide, and perform within organisations. His doctoral work sits at the intersection of cognitive performance, behavioural analysis, and organisational design: precisely the fields that determine whether AI actually gets adopted, trusted, and used effectively.

Most AI companies are built by engineers who optimise for technical benchmarks. Fano AI was built by someone who understands that intelligence — artificial or human — only delivers value when it is designed around how people actually think, behave, and make decisions under pressure.
